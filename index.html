A little about us

Good taste. Good company.

Paanagam celebrates the warmth of Tamil food culture — the comfort of a cup of filter coffee, the joy of sharing a bite, and the stories that happen around the table. Our idea is simple: make everyday food moments feel welcoming, familiar and full of character.
