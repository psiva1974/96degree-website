Paanagam celebrates the warmth of Tamil food culture — the comfort of a cup of filter coffee, the joy of sharing a bite, and the stories that happen around the table.

Our idea is simple: create a friendly place where familiar flavours and everyday moments come together. Come for a cup, stay for a conversation.
